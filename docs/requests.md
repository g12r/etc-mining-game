# What Emmett asked for

Every request, in his own words, in the order he made them, with what was built. The full conversation is in `transcript.md`.

| # | Emmett said | What was built |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "a realistic gold mine game ware you have to face realistic problems like ground water and cave ins" | The game itself: a gold mine with real mining problems. |
| 2 | Picked from choices: problems = "ground water, Bad air, Heat, Hard rock, Money and the mill"; look = "3D"; you are = "The miner" | A first-person 3D mine. Groundwater floods tunnels below about 60 ft and pumps drain it. Roofs cave in unless propped with timber sets. Fresh air only reaches about 10 blocks from an opening; a canary warns you and fans help. Rock gets hotter with depth. Hard rock needs a drill or dynamite. Ore sacks are crushed at the stamp mill and the assayer pays by the ounce. |
| 3 | "make it not blocky" | The rock is no longer cubes. The world is still stored as blocks, but the surface is rebuilt as a smooth mesh, so tunnels look carved. |
| 4 | "way more realistic i want it to be like you are in a real mine" | Darker mine lit by a headlamp that casts shadows, rough wet rock with gold specks in the quartz, dust in the lamp beam, an old mine entrance with rails, a cart and lanterns, timber sets, and sound. |
| 5 | "add rats thay are my favoret anamal" | Rats live in the mine. Press E next to one to feed it a crumb and it becomes a buddy that follows you. If the rats run away squeaking, a cave-in is coming. |
| 6 | "press the key D to use diynamite" | D is already "walk right", so he was asked to pick another key and chose **Q**. Q lights dynamite where you are looking. |
| 7 | "make it so you can damage the town" | The town can be dug and blasted. The townsfolk bill $1 for every board wrecked, and the carpenter repairs it after a while. Trees can be chopped for a timber set. |
| 8 | "no it dose not work fix it" (about Q for dynamite) | Three likely causes were fixed together: he had no dynamite, it needed too exact an aim, and the town was protected. Now you start with 3 sticks, Q drops the stick at your feet if you are not aiming at rock, and the town can be damaged. |
| 9 | "make it so you start with 100000000 dolers" | You start with $100,000,000. |
| 10 | "make relistic grafics and no floating bildings" | Better lighting and materials: sun shadows, a sky dome, grass that sways. Buildings and decorations now need something under them; dig or blast the support away and they fall down. |
| 11 | "make it so that pressing the key R places what ever is in yore hand" | R already placed things, but it silently did nothing when you held the pickaxe, had none of the item, or aimed at the sky. Now R always places what is in your hand (in front of your feet if you aren't aiming at anything) or says on screen why it can't. The start screen lists R. |
| 12 | "make the buildings have detailed exteriors and interiors" | Outside: a false front painted MINERS' SUPPLIES, shutters, side windows, corner boards, barrels, a bench and a hitching rail, a stovepipe and a boiler smokestack that smoke, and a roof vent on the mill. Inside the store: plank floor, shelves of cans, jars and boxes, a counter with a cash register, a shopkeeper who turns to look at you, picks and shovels on the wall, barrels, a powder crate and a stove. Inside the mill: a working five-stamp battery that pounds away, a flywheel and belt, a steam boiler with a glowing firebox, a copper amalgamation table, an ore pile, and the assayer's table with scales and gold bars. You can shop at the counter and sell to the assayer from inside. |
| 13 | "add canons that blow up on inpact" (asked twice) | Cannons, item 8. You start with 2 and the store sells more. Put one down with R, stand next to it and press E: it turns to whatever your crosshair is on, lifts its barrel for the arc, and fires an iron ball that explodes like dynamite where it lands. It won't fire at anything closer than 5 blocks, so the blast can't get you. |
| 14 | "add revolvers" | A six-shot revolver, item 9, held in your hand. Left click fires, R reloads (you start with 36 bullets and the store sells boxes of 50). It chips rock and wood but can't dig, sets off a lit stick of dynamite from far away, and knocks tin cans off a TARGET PRACTICE fence by the store; the cans go back up after a few seconds. Bullets never hurt rats or people. |
| 15 | "add horses" | Three saddled horses, Nugget (chestnut), Midnight (black) and Dusty (grey-white), tied up east of the store. Press E next to one to get on, ride with W A S D, hold Shift to gallop, Space to jump, E to get off. Legs walk, trot and gallop, the tail swishes, hooves clip-clop and they whinny. They stay where you leave them, and that is saved. If a roof is too low you hop off. |
| 16 | "make it so if you shoot a rock the number of gold in it pops upon yore screen" | Shoot a block and a number floats up from where the bullet hit: "0.42 oz gold $8" in gold for quartz, "No gold" for plain rock and dirt. Each block's gold is now fixed, so the number you see is what you get when you dig it. |
| 17 | "add mine elevators" | A mine elevator, item 0. You start with one and the store sells more. Put it down and the crew levels the spot, sinks a 2-by-2 shaft 60 feet down (just above the water table), and cuts a short station tunnel at the bottom. A timber headframe with a spinning sheave wheel stands over it, and a steel cage hangs on the cable. Step in and press E to go down, E again to stop at any level, E at the bottom to go up. Away from the cage, E rings for it. Bells ring like a real hoist. Dig the shaft deeper and the cage goes deeper too. |
