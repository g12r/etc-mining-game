This is **version 0.1** of **Untitled Emmett Game**: a realistic 3D gold mine where **you are the miner**. It has its own link, so Deepvein is still safe at its old one.

**How to play**
1. Click **Click to play**. Your mouse disappears, and moving it looks around. Press **Esc** to get it back.
2. Walk into town with **W A S D**. Stand at the **General Store** door and press **E** to buy ladders, timber and more. You start with $50.
3. Head out past the buildings onto your claim and **hold left click** to dig. **Right click** places whatever you're holding (keys **1–7**).
4. Find **quartz**, the white rock with gold specks. Every block goes into an **ore sack**.
5. Carry your sacks to the **Stamp Mill** and press **E**. It crushes the rock to get the gold out (that costs $1 a sack), and you get paid **$20 an ounce**.

**The real mining problems you asked for**
- 🪨 **Cave-ins:** a wide tunnel's roof starts to **creak**, dust falls and red cracks show up. Then it **comes down**, so put in timber posts!
- 💧 **Groundwater:** below about 60 feet, water seeps out of the rock and floods your tunnels. A **pump** drains it.
- 🐤 **Bad air:** your **canary** sings in good air. If it goes **silent**, the air is bad and your Air bar drops. A **fan** brings in fresh air. Dynamite smoke is bad air too!
- 🔥 **Heat:** it gets hotter the deeper you go, and your Heat bar fills up. Fans cool the tunnel down.
- ⛏️ **Hard rock:** deep rock is too hard for a pickaxe. Buy a **rock drill**, or use **dynamite**. Light it, then **run**!
- 💰 **Money:** if you pass out, the other miners carry you to town, but the doctor charges $10 and you lose the ore you were carrying.

Your mine saves in the browser, so you can come back to it. The **Full screen** button makes it fill the whole laptop.

I tested it: the town, digging, a cave-in, a tunnel flooding, the canary going silent, the heat, and selling ore at the mill all work. Some things to watch for:
- It's version 0.1, so things might feel too easy, too hard or a bit weird.
- Dig a **shaft straight down** and you'll need ladders to get back up. Place them against the wall.

Play it and tell me what's fun, what's boring and what you want to add next! What do you want to change first?
