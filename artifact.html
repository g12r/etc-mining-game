Untitled Emmett Game

You're a hard-rock gold miner. The old EMMETT No. 1 mine is in the mountain straight ahead. Buy gear at the General Store, follow the quartz, and haul your ore sacks to the Stamp Mill. Turn your sound on.

Cave-ins

A tunnel roof only holds so far from a wall: about 2 blocks in dirt, 3 in rock, 4 in hard rock. Past that it creaks, dust falls and it comes down. Put in timber sets: two posts and a cap beam across the tunnel.

Groundwater

Below about 60 feet, water seeps out of the rock and floods your tunnels. You can't breathe underwater. A pump drains everything near it.

Bad air

Fresh air only reaches about 10 blocks from an opening. Watch your canary: if it stops singing, get out, or set up a fan. Dynamite smoke is bad air too.

Heat

Rock gets hotter the deeper you go. Past about 95°F your heat bar fills up. A fan blowing cool air keeps you working.

Hard rock

Hard rock, and quartz deep down, won't break with a pick. Buy a rock drill or use dynamite. Light the fuse, then run!

Rats

Rats live in the mine. Press E next to one to give it a crumb, and it becomes your buddy and follows you. If the rats suddenly run away squeaking, a cave-in is coming!

The town

You can dig or blast anything in town, but the townsfolk bill you $1 for every board you wreck. The carpenter fixes it after a while. Chop a tree to get a timber set.

Money and the mill

Ore sacks aren't gold yet. The stamp mill crushes them for $1 a sack, and the assayer pays $20 an ounce. Deeper quartz holds more gold.
