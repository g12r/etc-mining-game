Untitled Emmett Game

You're a hard-rock gold miner. Buy gear at the General Store, dig down, find quartz with gold in it, and haul your ore sacks to the Stamp Mill.

Cave-ins

A tunnel roof only holds so far from a wall: about 2 blocks in dirt, 3 in rock, 4 in hard rock. Past that it creaks, dust falls and it comes down. Put in timber posts.

Groundwater

Below about 60 feet, water seeps out of the rock and floods your tunnels. You can't breathe underwater. A pump drains everything near it.

Bad air

Fresh air only reaches about 10 blocks from an opening. Watch your canary: if it stops singing, get out, or set up a fan. Dynamite smoke is bad air too.

Heat

Rock gets hotter the deeper you go. Past about 95°F your heat bar fills up. A fan blowing cool air keeps you working.

Hard rock

Hard rock, and quartz deep down, won't break with a pick. Buy a rock drill or use dynamite. Light the fuse, then run!

Money and the mill

Ore sacks aren't gold yet. The stamp mill crushes them for $1 a sack, and the assayer pays $20 an ounce. Deeper quartz holds more gold.
